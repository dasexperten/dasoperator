Line accent stripe

4 px top bar in the product-line color. The only fully-bleed color on the face.

Corporate lockup

Ribbons + wordmark. Always at the top. Min. height 6 mm on print.

Product wordmark

Archivo 900 in the line color. Descriptor below in Archivo Narrow, tracked wide.

Primary claim

Two lines max. Archivo 700. Schwarz on paper. No exclamation marks.

Ingredient molecules

Coloured dots echo the claim's active compounds. Never more than three.

Volume readout

Manrope. Bottom-right. The only proportional-numerals readout on the face.
